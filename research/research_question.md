# Research Background
Auditing is a complex, resource-intensive process involving risk assessments, evidence collection, verification, documentation, and professional judgment. 
Increasing regulatory complexity, growing volumes of digital evidence and limited organizational resources create challenges for conventional audit planning and execution. 
Artificial Intelligence (AI) offers opportunities to improve risk prediction, evidence analysis, and audit task automation. Mathematical optimization can support effective audit scheduling and resource allocation.
However, integrating predictive intelligence, optimization, and autonomous task execution into coherent, adaptive, and quality-constrained audit framework requires further investigation.
# Research Problem
The central research problem is how to optimize audit planning and execution under limited resources and uncertainty while maintaining mandatory audit requirements, evidence traceability, and audit quality. 
In particular, audit planning must account for multiple interdependent factors:
- Different levels of risk and task complexity.
- Uncertain audit execution time and resource requirements.
- Limited audit availability and qualifications.
- Mandatory audit activities and evidence requirements.
- Unexpected findings and changes during audit execution.

The challenge is not simply to automate individual activities, but to develop an integrated decision-support framework that connects risk predictions, resource allocation and evidence-grounded execution.
# Preliminary Research Gap
Existing research investigates several relevant areas, including:
- Risk-based audit planning and prioritization.
- Mathematical optimization for audit scheduling and resource allocation.
- AI-supported risk assessment and audit analytics.
- Automated evidence analysis and agent-based workflows.
- Human- AI collaboration and accountable decision-making.
# Main Research Question
How can integrated, uncertainty-aware AI framework combining predictive intelligence, quality-constrained multi-objective optimization, and agent-based execution dynamically optimize audit planning and resource allocation while maintaining audit quality, mandatory requirements, and evidence traceability?
# Sub Research Questions
- Predictive Intelligence
  How can predictive AI estimate audit risk, task complexity, and execution effort, including the uncertainty associated with these predictions?
- Mathematical Optimization:
  How can a multi-objective optimization model allocate limited audit resources and schedule interdependent audit tasks while satisfying mandatory audit requirements and quality constraints?
- Agent-Based Execution:
  How can AI agents coordinate and execute selected audit activities through structured evidence retrieval, automated verification, and evidence-grounded documentation under human supervision?
- Dynamic Adaption:
  How can execution feedback, unexpected findings, and updated risk information be incorporated into a dynamic mechanism for revising audit prioritize and re-optimizing the remaining audit plan?
- Experimental Evaluation:
  To what extend does the integrated framework improve audit efficiency, resource utilization, risk-weighted coverage, and evidence quality compared with conventional and partially automated approaches?
