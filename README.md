# ReguAssure-AI
An integrated hybrid AI framework for intelligent audit process optimization.
# Overview
ReguAssure AI is a research exploring how Artificial Intelligence and mathematical optimization can improve the efficiency, resource allocation, and quality of audit processes. 
The project processes an integrated framework combining predictive AI, multi objective optimization, and AI-agent based execution, with human oversight at its core.
# Research problem
Auditing processes often involve times consuming evidence reviews, complex risk assessments, and limited human an operational resources. 
The central challenge to improve audit efficiency and resource utilization without compromising audit quality evidence traceability, or regulatory requirements. 
# Research Objective
To design, implement and evaluate a hybrid AI framework that optimizes audit planning and execution by:
Predicting audit riss, task complexity, and expected effort.
Prioritizing audit activities and allocating limited sources.
Automating selected audit procedures through AI agents.
Maintaining evidence traceability and human validation.
# Proposed Framework
| Component | Main Function |
|---|---|
| Predictive AI | Risk and effort estimation |
| Optimization Engine | Audit planning and resource allocation |
| AI Agent | Coordination and execution of selected tasks |
| Audit Engine | Evidence analysis and automated checks |
| Human Oversight | Review, validation, and approval |
# Research Application
The initial case study focuses on quality auditing in medical software development. 
The framework is designed as a general approach to audit processes optimization, with medical software serving as an application context.
# Evaluation
The proposed framework will be evaluated against conventional and partially automated approaches using metrics such as:
- Audit execution time
- Resource utilization
- Risk-detection performance
- Evidence completeness and traceability
- Audit finding accuracy
# Research Question
How can an integrated AI framework combining predictive intelligence, multi-objective optimization, and agent-based execution improve audit efficiency and resource allocation while maintaining audit quality and evidence traceability?
# Project Status
Current phase: 
The architecture, optimization model, implementationstartegy, and evaluation methodology are under development. The proposed framework has not yet been experimentally validated.
