# ReguAssure-AI
An integrated hybrid AI framework for intelligent audit process optimization.
# Overview
ReguAssure AI is a research exploring how Artificial Intelligence and mathematical optimization can improve the efficiency, resource allocation, and quality of audit processes. 
The project processes an integrated framework combining predictive AI, multi objective optimization, and AI-agent based execution, with human oversight at its core.
# Research problem
Auditing processes often involve times consuming evidence reviews, complex risk assessments, and limited human an operational resources. 
The central challenge to improve audit efficiency and resource utilization without compromising audit quality evidence traceability, or regulatory requirements. 
# Research Objective
To design, implement and evaluate a hybrid AI framework that optimizes audit planning and execution by:
Predicting audit riss, task complexity, and expected effort.
Prioritizing audit activities and allocating limited sources.
Automating selected audit procedures through AI agents.
Maintaining evidence traceability and human validation.
